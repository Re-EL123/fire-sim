Site briefing

A fire has been detected on the construction site. Assess the situation and follow the guided extinguisher procedure.

Before you fight any fire: raise the alarm, follow your site emergency procedure and evacuate if required. Only attempt to fight a small, contained fire if it is safe to do so and you have received the appropriate training. If in doubt, get out.

Desktop controls: WASD to move, mouse to look, E to collect the extinguisher, and hold the left mouse button to discharge after preparing it. Press E at the red call point first to raise the alarm, and keep a safe distance (3–5 m) from the heat. Press Esc at any time to pause.

Meta Quest 2 / Quest 3 (WebXR): open this page in Quest Browser and tap Enter VR (bottom-right). The left thumbstick moves, the right snap-turns, pressing a thumbstick teleports, the grip collects the extinguisher and pulls the safety pin, and the trigger discharges. Objectives and meters ride on your left wrist.

Training concept only. Fire behaviour, equipment operation and assessment criteria are illustrative and must be reviewed by a qualified fire-safety professional before use as actual training. This is not a substitute for accredited fire-safety training.
