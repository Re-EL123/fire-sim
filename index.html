Explore a construction-site scenario, locate the extinguisher and interact with a simulated fire. This is an early visual prototype showing the intended direction for the Re-EL firefighting training experience.

Desktop controls: WASD to move, mouse to look, E to collect the extinguisher, and hold the left mouse button to simulate discharge after collecting it. Optionally press E at the red call point first to raise the alarm, and keep a safe distance (3–5 m) from the heat.

Training concept only. Fire behaviour, equipment operation and assessment criteria are illustrative and must be reviewed by a qualified fire-safety professional before use as actual training.
